Numismatic Ledger

Welcome to CoinTrack

Keep track of every coin you own, organized into collections: what it is, what you paid, and what it's worth today.

A quick tour takes about 30 seconds.

Organize

Build your collections

  • Sort into collectionsGroup coins into collections in the side panel, or view All coins. Tap + New collection to start another.
  • Tell its storyEach collection shows its name, description and start date up top. Use Edit to change them.
  • Pick a highlight coinTap the ☆ on any coin to feature it at the top of its collection.

Record

Log every coin

  • Capture the detailsYear, mint, grade, price paid and value, plus which collection it belongs to. Names you've used before are suggested as you type.
  • Photograph itObverse, reverse and receipt photos are kept with each coin.

Explore

Track, find and share

  • Know what it's worthSide panel stats follow the collection you pick: coin count, value, gain, top coin and how long you've been collecting.
  • Find coins fastSearch, filter by type or grader, and sort by value, gain, year or grade.
  • Share a coin sheetDownload any coin as a clean, printable PDF.

Private by design

Your coins stay in this browser

No account and no servers. That also means your collection won't follow you to other devices, and clearing your browser data erases it.

Use Export JSON Backup in the side menu regularly, and Import Backup to restore or move it.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays locally inside your browser's private storage. No accounts, external databases, or third-party servers are used.
How does the Import feature work?
Clicking Import lets you select a previously exported .json backup file to restore your entire collection, including all coin details and photos.
Are there monthly fees or hidden costs?
No. CoinTrack.io is 100% free with no subscriptions or recurring fees.
How do I backup my collection?
Click Export JSON Backup in the side menu to download a single backup file containing all your coin records and images. The side menu shows when you last backed up, and CoinTrack reminds you when a new backup is due.
Can I install CoinTrack like an app?
Yes. On iPhone or iPad, open cointrack.io in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or in Chrome and Edge on a computer, use Install app at the bottom of the page or the install icon in the address bar. Once installed it opens in its own window and works without an internet connection.
How do I verify a graded coin?
Add the cert number from the slab label when you add or edit a coin. For PCGS and NGC coins, the coin's details then show a Verify link that opens the grading service's certificate lookup. NGC's lookup also needs the numeric grade, e.g. MS-63.
What are the Wishlist and Sold lists?
When adding or editing a coin, choose whether it's in your collection, on your wishlist or sold. Wishlist coins track a target price; sold coins keep what you paid and what you sold for, so you can see your realized gain. Only coins in your collection count toward the totals in the side panel.
How do I give my collection to an insurer or open it in a spreadsheet?
At the bottom of the side menu, Download CSV opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, and Inventory PDF creates a printable list with photos, grades, cert numbers and values. Both cover the collection you have selected, or everything when All coins is selected.
