Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays locally inside your browser's private storage. No accounts, external databases, or third-party servers are used.

How does the Import feature work?

Clicking Import Backup lets you select a previously exported .json backup file to restore your entire collection, including all coin details and photos.

Are there monthly fees or hidden costs?

No. CoinTrack.io is 100% free and open to use without any subscriptions or recurring fees.

How do I backup my collection?

Click the Export Backup button at the top to download a single backup file containing all your coin records and images.
