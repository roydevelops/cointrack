Welcome to CoinTrack.io

Your secure, private, local numismatic portfolio ledger. Keep track of your valuable coin collections, certification holders, purchase prices, and estimated market values with zero monthly fees.

  • Precious Metal & Portfolio Tracking: Aggregate holdings count and monitor estimated market values vs. acquisition costs.
  • Certification & Slab Registry: Log third-party grading services (PCGS, NGC, ANACS, ICG) with specific grades and serial numbers.
  • Dual-Side Photo Archiving: Store high-resolution obverse and reverse face photos alongside transaction receipts and certification papers.
  • 100% Private & Self-Hosted: Your collection data stays securely in your browser with zero subscription fees and instant JSON backups.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays locally inside your browser's private storage. No accounts, external databases, or third-party servers are used.
How does the Import feature work?
Clicking Import lets you select a previously exported .json backup file to restore your entire collection, including all coin details and photos.
Are there monthly fees or hidden costs?
No. CoinTrack.io is 100% free with no subscriptions or recurring fees.
How do I backup my collection?
Click the Export button at the top to download a single backup file containing all your coin records and images.
